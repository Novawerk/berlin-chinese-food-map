Privacy Policy隐私政策

Data Storage数据存储

Berlin Chinese Food Map stores your personal data (favorites, preferences) locally on your device. Restaurant data may be fetched from our open-source database.柏林中餐地图将你的个人数据（收藏、偏好）存储在设备本地。餐厅数据可能从我们的开源数据库获取。

Data Collection数据收集

We do not collect personal data. Specifically:我们不收集个人数据：

  • No user accounts required无需用户账号
  • No analytics or tracking没有分析或追踪
  • No advertising没有广告
  • Location is only used for map display, never transmitted位置信息仅用于地图显示，绝不会被传输

Community Contributions社区贡献

Restaurant data and reviews are community-contributed via GitHub. Contributors are identified by their GitHub username only.餐厅数据和评论通过 GitHub 由社区贡献。贡献者仅通过其 GitHub 用户名标识。
